# M2.4 Snow technology-flag component reuse proof

The four original Snow technology-building flag members can reuse already accepted Temperate materials. `audit-snow-tech-flag-reuse.ts` verifies current imported original bytes, canvas/trim geometry, every index in all 16 nonempty body frames, used fixed palette entries and every selected accepted 2X/4X payload hash. The proof is `.artifacts/m2-4/snow-tech-flag-reuse-eligibility-v2.json`. Original Hospital and Airport members are raw-identical to their donors. Oil and Outpost have additional original zero-size empty slots; their 16 visible frames remain exactly source-identical. The matched fixed palette counts are respectively 40, 35, 54 and 41.

This corrects a possible misreading of the earlier semantic audit: its first frame count is Temperate, its second Snow. Oil and Outpost Snow have 32 logical body slots, with 16 nonempty donor-matching frames plus 16 original empty slots. Hospital retains 32 slots, including its exact original empty second half. Airport has 16 body slots and 16 separate original shadow slots. No shadow or empty slot is reinterpreted as missing authored art. All original native looping and shadow declarations remain unchanged.

The audit is read-only and counts no new coverage. No resource selection or prepared index changes. Complete Snow technology families still require their new Snow main bodies and linked animation members. When assembled, the four flag members require source-pinned Snow metadata and fresh preparation; accepted material PNGs remain exact, and Oil/Outpost metadata must retain their original additional empty slots. No cache can be relabeled to match the new source. These component proofs avoid reauthoring accepted flag art and repeating its complete visual matrix. Windows is not run.
