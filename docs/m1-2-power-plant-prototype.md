## Post-completion visual review amendment

After checkpoint `8e75955`, the user reported that the original-versus-authored gameplay comparison shows a smaller improvement than expected from the generated images. Infantry appears somewhat better, but also falls short of the source-image impression. The user then observed that the same-HD-art 1080p-versus-4K comparison shows a more obvious improvement, and requested returning to the main conversation to decide the next step. The comparisons isolate different variables. `game-review-v1/original-vs-hd.png` holds 4K output constant and changes artwork. `same-art-output-density.png` holds accepted HD artwork constant and changes the output buffer from 1920x1080 to 3840x2160 at equal logical size. **Both right-hand panels use the exact same HD 4K source screenshot and crop.** The latter's stronger perceived difference therefore comes from its different left-hand control, not a better HD candidate. Both boards enlarge the crop to 3x logical size; neither represents additional detail generated by enlargement. Large generated-image previews are a third viewing condition and are not direct native-size gameplay comparisons.

Current code confirms nearest-neighbor texture sampling with no mipmaps, intersection of generated alpha with the upscaled original pixel silhouette, and conversion of marked owner-color regions to 16 palette shades. The normal body frame is 84x92 native pixels and its 4x export is 336x368. Fixed apparent size limits the output pixels available to show that source detail. These mechanisms can affect detail retention, edges and material appearance, but their individual perceptual contributions have **not** been isolated with controlled alternatives. The prior conversational explanation was a diagnosis of plausible contributors, not a measured attribution or proof of a quality ceiling. It also does not establish that changing filtering alone will meet expectations; base-color filtering and discrete remap data require separate consideration.

The sample-direction and complete-candidate approvals remain historical facts and their receipts are unchanged. They do not establish satisfaction with the final in-game visual result. Preserve the technical PASS results while leaving final visual satisfaction open. No pack, shader, gameplay rule, screenshot or prior acceptance receipt is changed by this documentation amendment.
