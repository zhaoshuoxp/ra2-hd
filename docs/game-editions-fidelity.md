# Retail-fidelity review checklist

Status: `USER_REVIEW_PENDING`. This is a scoped list of known differences and unverified details, based on the current source and native evidence. It is not a claim that every retail interaction has been audited. Campaigns remain deferred. An automated gameplay pass does not accept these differences for the user.

| Area | Current behavior | Classification and visible consequence |

## Corrected bugs, not proposed differences to accept

R2 corrects the selector's side bands, YR's shifted health/rank SHP indices, and the obsolete temperate-only restriction on compatible HD resources. Healthy units use green health pips, intermediate health yellow and critical health red in both editions. Queue overlays, bold counts/status and original production/deployment audio from R1 remain retained.

R3 fixes the absent-weapon guard-scan crash and integrates main's 22 accepted M1.7 unit families, with source-verified reuse on compatible dry theaters. SQD and YURIPR retain their Original exceptions; YR-only units outside that batch and snow unit variants are not newly authored. Industrial Plant (NAINDP), Battle Bunker (NABNKR), distinct snow building variants and incompatible NAPOWR construction variants remain building HD gaps. See [R3 verification](game-editions-feedback-r3.md) and the [M1.6 continuation prompt](m1-6-soviet-building-gaps-execution-prompt.md).
