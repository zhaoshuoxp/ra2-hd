## Bugs, causes and reusable solutions

| Symptom or failed approach | Cause and accepted correction | Guidance for subsequent factions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New Allied vehicles looked much blurrier than Rhino, including lossless screenshots | Allied VXL used 2x atlases and a forced four-texel footprint. Near-opaque alpha around 253/255 entered the coverage branch, which averaged interior detail. Use the Rhino one-texel minimum and appropriate master-derived 4x export. | Inspect the actual material, effective atlas density, alpha threshold and derivative footprint. Video compression or nominal Nearest filters alone do not explain the complete rendering path. |
| Soviet units also lost detail | General Soviet VXL derived a roughly four-texel floor from authored/native size; Conscript inherited a native-pixel-width smooth-coverage floor. Both HD paths now use one texel while normal derivative antialiasing remains. | Audit each archetype, child and rotor path. Avoid global sharpening or disabling ordinary antialiasing to compensate for a forced footprint. |
| Bright or stepped ground-colored gaps between vehicles and shadows | Filtering initially spread the silhouette, but a second defect remained: bridge-only native depth-mask visibility evaluated to `undefined`. Three.js skipped only explicit `false`, so an invisible original voxel outline masked the shadow receiver. Boolean coercion fixes the shared path; Rhino had no such bridge-only mesh. | Test rendering visibility semantics, not only texture alpha. Same-frame default/legacy/forced-mask-removal comparisons isolate an invisible occluder without repainting artwork. Preserve legitimate bridge cases. |
| DLPH moved sideways and emitted attacks from its side | Its source facing order is clockwise; generic vehicle SHP selection is counterclockwise. Reverse only the dolphin's visual-facing mapping. | Derive each member's facing contract from native data. Preserve animation phase, motion, target direction and weapon effects. |
| DEST/CARRIER/AEGIS/LCRF hulls or deck aircraft were clipped | Registered sprite depth extended below the water clipping surface. Reuse camera-ray depth recovery, retaining screen projection and native sinking/pitch. | A successful HD binding does not prove full visible hull coverage. Inspect waterline, deck, child aircraft and concealed/sinking states. |
| ORCA/BEAG disappeared after returning to the airfield | Dock registration occurs before descent completes; final aircraft depth could be behind airport body/bib/animation surfaces. Apply occupied-dock depth recovery along camera rays only after actual parking. | Observe final descent, parking visibility and reload. An early dock flag or successful sortie is insufficient. |
| Building or infantry shadows remained jagged despite 4x bodies | Retail SHP shadows are separate binary indexed frames or palette-12 pixels embedded in body frames. Body atlas resolution does not increase these native masks. Use bounded fractional native-pixel coverage and distinguish embedded black shadow pixels from boots/body detail/remap. | Test both SHP shadow routes and batching modes. Dynamic model-shadow quality is a separate mechanism. Clamp filtering to the current frame to prevent neighboring-frame leakage. |
| Some red panels failed to recolor, especially death/damage poses and oblique naval/Soviet views | Authored paint extended beyond the native owner-index support region. Build complete authored material masks across every selected action/heading. GAGAP/ATESLA retail `Remapable=no` metadata disagreed with actual owner-palette use. | Validate normal/damaged/construction/death/deployed/child members and multiple non-red owners. Use palette evidence and material semantics; red fire, blood, rust, skin and fixed insignia are not owner paint. |
| Disabling superweapons also removed battle labs and advanced units | The filter disabled every structure carrying a shell-disableable power; YR battle labs carry Force Shield. Restrict it to the six primary power types, two per faction. | Test reachable prerequisites and unchanged unit sets with the option both ways, not only the presence of superweapon icons. |
| SHAD rotor did not animate; its front/rear geometry was then misoriented | Retail SHAD omits optional rotor metadata. Add native joints, but do not copy SCHP's axes: SHAD has two local-Z horizontal rotors; SCHP has local-Z main and local-X vertical tail. | Measure section bounds/HVA and use native parent attitude, spin timing and explicit metadata precedence. Shared helpers do not imply identical rotor layouts. |
| Helicopter belly disappeared in side/rear views | The shared depth envelope omitted registered component-origin offsets and clipped geometry before rasterization. Expand it around all registered images after registration, retaining a shared surface. | Verify full UV rectangle coverage for every registered component. Culling, rotor visibility or disabling depth writes cannot restore vertices already clipped away. |
| Rotor hubs orbited their mounts or appeared detached | Painted hub centers differed from native axes. Normalized hub anchors align the artwork, but per-bearing rear-height tweaks still failed visual review. Restore one fixed native-parent offset and add stationary bearing housings. | Check axes, painted anchors and stationary support separately. Update joint matrices after controlled quaternion changes. A hub-axis numeric PASS is not visual acceptance. |

Current selected manifests live in `.local/hd-packs/`; desktop catalogs and runtime identities must be updated together using immutable new revisions.

The hardware gate was tested on Apple M2, 10 GPU cores, 24 GB unified memory, ANGLE/Metal, at 3840x2160. Complete YR temperate HD textures measured 1,009.75 MiB; RA2 temperate measured 938.47 MiB. A further 512 MiB texture probe reported no GL errors/context loss, with synchronized p95 7.23 ms in the YR case. Live p95 was 19.78 ms for YR and 19.11 ms for RA2. A matched changed-family fixture reduced storage by 70% at 2x. These are bounded engine allocation/timing measurements, not physical VRAM readings or universal sustained-battle guarantees.

Adequate masters were re-exported; the 24 deficient families were regenerated from accepted references, preserving appearance and old 2x bytes. Four inherited CARRIER bearings have the earlier documented 1.02–1.35x master enlargement. Their 4x atlas density is not evidence of newly recovered detail; this is a retained, accepted sampling limitation, not an open implementation blocker. Semantic masks and native shadow data are control data, not authored 4x RGB.
